\begin{frame}{What to include in your pre-registrations}
\scriptsize
\begin{itemize}
\item Research question and hypothesis
\begin{itemize}\scriptsize
\item How does this project contribute to greater scientific knowledge?
\item What are you expecting to find?
\end{itemize}
\item (light) Literature review 
\item Study population / procedure for obtaining sample frame. Inclusion criteria?
\begin{itemize}\scriptsize
\item Further information about context?
\end{itemize}
\item Is informed consent obtained? If not, why not?
\item What is being manipulated? Be specific. 
\item Randomization procedures/unit of randomization. 
\item Data collection: how/when is data collected? What is the unit of measurement? (individual? village? school? multiple of these?)
\item What outcomes and covariates are measured? How are they measured: surveys, behavioral games, behavioral measures, administrative data? Any justifications for measurement choices. 
\item Analyses/statistical tests? (Just a general idea, but describe e.g., any subgroup analyses or tests for heterogeneous treatment effects. Which groups are you interested in, and what do you expect to find?)
\item Ethical considerations
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}
